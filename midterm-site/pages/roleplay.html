Rainy Evening at the Hospital

Participants: Jocelyn Ash, Estella Deavor, & Nyx-7

Jocelyn Ash

The soft patter of rain against the old, rusted window creates a somewhat cozy ambiance as Jocelyn leans back in her chair, swirling a cup of herbal tea. She tries to ignore the chill in the air, focusing instead on the mug's warmth and the comforting aroma of chamomile and mint.

Estella Deavor

Estella, ever the observant one, notices Jocelyn's attempt to stay warm and offers a sly smile. "You know," she says, adjusting her glasses, "a little rain never hurt anyone. But if you want, I might have a jacket you can borrow."

Nyx-7

He adjusts his monocle as he glances over at the two, his fingers tapping lightly on the table. "Rain or shine, the hospital never sleeps," he remarks, his voice carrying a hint of amusement.
